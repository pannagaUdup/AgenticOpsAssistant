# AI Operations Assistant — Product Requirements

## Problem

Operations teams need to investigate customer accounts across account data, interaction history, and policy documents before deciding on a next action. The information is distributed and manual investigation is slow and inconsistent.

## Users

Operations and support users who need grounded account investigations and safe follow-up recommendations.

## Core use cases

- Investigate an account from a natural-language question.
- Retrieve account and interaction history.
- Search policy documents with citations.
- Recommend a next action with confidence and evidence.
- Create a follow-up task only after human approval.

## Out of scope

- Autonomous customer communication.
- Production customer data.
- Unapproved side effects.
- Financial, legal, or medical decision-making.

## Success criteria

Recommendations are structured, evidence-backed, auditable, and safe to retry.
